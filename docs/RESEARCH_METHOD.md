# Research method

1. **Identify the input.** Record title, region, language, revision, size, and
   cryptographic hashes. Keep the binary outside Git.
2. **State the question.** Make the unit of work narrow and falsifiable.
3. **Preserve the procedure.** Record tools, versions, commands, environment,
   and address conventions.
4. **Capture evidence.** Prefer small tables and manifests with source offsets.
5. **Separate interpretation.** Label observed, derived, inferred, and unknown
   claims.
6. **Reproduce independently.** Re-run from a clean checkout and the same
   identified input.
7. **Promote stable knowledge.** Move confirmed target-neutral methods here;
   keep title-specific facts in the target repository.

Addresses must declare file-offset versus CPU-address semantics. Hashes use
lowercase hexadecimal. Reports must state whether ranges are inclusive or
half-open.
